# SDCDP requirements for chartered clubs

Use this reference for membership, chartering, meetings, endorsements, Associates, and reporting. It summarizes the SDCDP Bylaws and Policies and Procedures verified September 27, 2026; consult the linked originals for controlling text.

## Charter and membership

- Charter annually with SDCDP.
- Maintain at least 20 voting members to charter and at least 20 unique voting members for an Associate.
- Voting members must be registered Democrats in San Diego County or state an intention to register that way as soon as eligible.
- Non-voting membership may include people registered with another preference.
- At least two-thirds of all members must be registered Democrats or pledged to register when eligible.
- Only registered or pledged Democrats may vote on club business or serve as officers.
- Membership must be open without the discrimination prohibited by SDCDP rules.
- The bylaws must state a process for selecting potential SDCDP and CDP representatives.

## Meetings and records

- Meet individually at least once each quarter.
- Provide every meeting's date, time, and location to SDCDP at least 14 days beforehand.
- Email the Director of Clubs and SDCDP copies of notices, agendas, and minutes for business and endorsement meetings.
- Conduct club endorsement votes individually, presided over by a club officer, unless the SDCDP Executive Board permits a specified joint meeting.

## Club endorsements

- Endorse only registered Democrats.
- A non-Democratic candidate may be rated only **Qualified** or **Unacceptable**.
- Give the full membership and SDCDP at least 14 days' notice of the races under consideration.
- Make reasonable attempts to notify and invite every declared candidate whom the Party has confirmed is a registered Democrat at least five business days before the meeting.
- Email a copy of the candidate notice to the Director of Clubs and SDCDP.
- Request candidate information from the Party office at least seven business days before the meeting.
- Allow all local Democratic candidates to speak or use a surrogate. In a race without a Democratic incumbent, consider all Democratic candidates.
- Clearly distinguish endorsed candidates from candidates rated Qualified.
- Wherever an endorsement is mentioned, state clearly that it is the club's action and not an official CDP or SDCDP endorsement.
- Noncompliance may cause the SDCDP Executive Board to suspend the club's endorsement voting rights until resolved.

## SDCDP Associate endorsement direction

- The Associate may vote in an Area endorsement recommendation only after a valid club vote and submission of the required Party form at least 48 hours before the Area Caucus.
- When the club endorsed a candidate, vote for that candidate in every round where the candidate appears.
- When the club adopted No Endorsement, vote No Endorsement in every round.
- When neither a candidate nor No Endorsement met the club threshold, record No Consensus and abstain.
- Follow any valid, specific contingency instruction when the endorsed candidate is absent from a later ballot.
- For a Friendly Incumbent Endorsement, follow the club's properly noticed direction and timely form.
- A president-designated substitute must be named on the submitted form.

## Charter-year administration

- Submit the constitution/bylaws, membership list, application, fee or waiver request, and proof of compliance through the current electronic process.
- The published renewal deadline is November 15, with other timing requirements stated in the current Policies and Procedures; verify operational dates before advising a filing.
- Report officer changes to the Director of Clubs within 30 days of the change or election, whichever comes first.
- After a bylaws change, submit the new bylaws and an outline of changes within 30 days. The charter is subject to Executive Board review.
- Use of “Democratic” in fundraising solicitation is subject to the approval described in the SDCDP Bylaws.
