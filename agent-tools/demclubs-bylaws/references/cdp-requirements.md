# CDP rules that expressly affect county-chartered clubs

Use this reference when the club wants representatives at California Democratic Party pre-endorsing conferences. It summarizes CDP Bylaws Article VIII, Section 3(g), verified September 27, 2026.

## Club and roster eligibility

- The club must have been chartered in the year before the pre-endorsing conferences, and the chartering authority must certify the charter's validity.
- The club must submit its roster of members in good standing who are registered Democrats to the chartering authority, applicable CDP Regional Director, and CDP Secretary by the deadline announced by the State Chair.
- The club's bylaws or rules must define **member in good standing**.
- Only members in good standing as of the State Chair's announced cutoff may appear on the roster. That cutoff is 14 days before the roster deadline.
- The President, Secretary, or Treasurer certifies good-standing status.

## Representative selection and eligibility

- Representatives must come from the certified roster.
- The overall list must follow the CDP Equal Division Rule to the extent possible.
- A member may represent only one club.
- A representative may not also participate in that conference by qualifying as a Democratic State Central Committee or County Committee member.
- The bylaws must either state a selection process in which only members in good standing participate, or representatives must be selected at a duly noticed club meeting by the members in good standing present and voting.
- Representatives may vote only in districts where they reside.
- Proxy voting is prohibited.

## Allocation

After voter-file validation, members belonging to multiple clubs receive fractional value across those clubs. A club with at least 20 submitted members and a fractional value of at least 15 within an Assembly District receives representatives by dividing the fractional value by 20 and rounding to the nearest whole number.

Use the State Chair's currently announced dates. Do not hard-code an election-cycle deadline from an older notice.

## Boundary

Do not import CDP Article X requirements for organizations chartered directly by the CDP into ordinary SDCDP club bylaws unless the club actually seeks or holds a state charter. County Central Committees remain the primary chartering authorities for local organizations.
