# Authority and sources

Rule-pack version: **0.2.1**  
Last verified: **September 27, 2026**  
Jurisdiction: **San Diego County Democratic Party**

## Order of authority

Use the controlling law and current Party rules first, followed by the club's validly adopted bylaws and policies. The DemClubs synthesis and the older Club Manual model are drafting aids, not independent authority.

For ordinary San Diego County clubs:

1. Applicable law.
2. Current California Democratic Party rules that expressly apply to county-chartered clubs or their representatives.
3. Current SDCDP Bylaws.
4. Current SDCDP Policies and Procedures.
5. The club's bylaws and adopted policies.
6. Recommended safeguards, common practice, and club preferences.

Do not apply requirements for organizations chartered directly by the CDP to an ordinary SDCDP-chartered club unless the CDP text expressly reaches county-chartered clubs. In particular, CDP Article X state-charter membership rules are not the default charter rules for a county club; CDP Article VIII's pre-endorsing representative provisions do expressly reach qualifying county-chartered clubs.
