# Interview and drafting method

Use this method for a complete creation or review engagement.

## Decision sequence

Work through the topics in this order, skipping only matters the user has already resolved clearly:

1. Voting membership eligibility.
2. Dues and hardship access.
3. Definition of member in good standing.
4. When a member becomes eligible to vote.
5. Minimum meeting cadence.
6. Membership quorum.
7. Remote participation.
8. Notice for consequential actions.
9. Authority to call special meetings.
10. Heightened quorum for elections and endorsements.
11. Required officers.
12. Officer terms.
13. Nomination and election procedure.
14. Officer duties.
15. Officer removal and due process.
16. Filling vacancies.
17. Executive-board authority.
18. Executive-board quorum.
19. Standing and temporary committees.
20. Endorsement threshold.
21. Required endorsement process.
22. Financial controls.
23. Member discipline.
24. Selection of official Party representatives.
25. Representative duties and reporting.
26. SDCDP Associate endorsement instructions.
27. Bylaws amendment process.
28. Parliamentary authority.

## Presenting a decision

State why the choice matters, its authority classification, and its source basis. Give two or three workable alternatives. Recommend one only when there is a defensible operational reason. Describe the consequence of each alternative in plain language.

Always offer an editable custom option pre-populated with the recommended language. If the user edits required language in a way that appears noncompliant, identify the conflict and ask for resolution rather than silently restoring or accepting it.

## Recommended defaults

Defaults are starting points, not hidden decisions:

- Objective good-standing criteria with a confidential dues waiver and no attendance penalty unless the club deliberately chooses one.
- A scalable membership quorum with a minimum headcount.
- Full remote participation when the club can administer identity and private voting reliably.
- At least 14 days' notice for consequential actions.
- Open nominations, private contested ballots, and a majority runoff.
- A board limited to routine administration consistent with membership action.
- A 60% endorsement threshold and a visible No Endorsement option.
- Two-person approval for unbudgeted spending and regular financial reporting.
- Written notice, an opportunity to respond, and a member-controlled disciplinary appeal or decision.
- Member selection of representatives at a duly noticed meeting.
- Two-thirds vote with at least 14 days' notice for amendments.
- Current Robert's Rules as a subordinate gap-filler, not as a replacement for the bylaws.

## Draft structure

A complete draft should ordinarily cover:

1. Name.
2. Purpose.
3. Membership, good standing, dues, and voting eligibility.
4. Officers, elections, duties, removal, and vacancies.
5. Meetings, notice, quorum, remote participation, and voting.
6. Executive board.
7. Committees.
8. Candidate endorsements and issue positions.
9. Party representatives and roster certification.
10. Finances, records, conflicts, and discipline.
11. Annual charter compliance and amendments.
12. Parliamentary authority, adoption, and dissolution.

Every generated clause must trace to either a controlling requirement or a recorded club decision. Mark unresolved text visibly; do not fill it with an invented policy choice.

## Review output

Separate findings into:

- **Recorded / covered** — language exists, but confirm it is internally consistent.
- **Required decision missing** — the draft cannot be considered ready.
- **Potential conflict** — club language appears inconsistent with a controlling rule or another clause.
- **Review recommended** — a safeguard or clarity improvement, not a Party requirement.

Keyword presence alone is not proof of sufficiency. Treat the included validator as triage and complete a substantive reading before presenting conclusions.
