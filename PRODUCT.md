# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: React, TypeScript, and Vite, deployed as a static Netlify site with a narrowly scoped Netlify Function for optional AI assistance.

## Users

The primary users are officers and bylaws committees of San Diego County Democratic clubs. They may be creating bylaws for a new club or repairing an existing document before member review and adoption. Future jurisdiction profiles will support other California Democratic clubs and general membership organizations.

## Product Purpose

DemClubs turns governance decisions into a traceable bylaws draft. It interviews a club, identifies missing or conflicting provisions, explains viable alternatives, and produces an editable draft plus a separate recommendations report. Success means a club reaches internal review with every required decision visible and no hidden AI-authored policy choices.

## Positioning

The product combines a deterministic, source-backed governance rule pack with an adaptive interview. AI may clarify or tailor language, but requirements, statuses, selected thresholds, and generated clauses remain controlled by versioned rules and explicit user decisions.

## Operating Context

Users work from an existing PDF, DOCX, TXT file, pasted text, or a blank start. They move through short governance sections, compare alternatives, resolve contradictions, edit generated text, and export DOCX and Markdown. Club work is often completed over several meetings, so the browser saves drafts locally and provides explicit import, export, and deletion controls.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Launch jurisdiction: San Diego County Democratic Party.
- Complete English and Spanish interface and output.
- Text-based PDF, DOCX, TXT, and pasted-text input; scanned-document OCR is outside v1.
- Readiness profile instead of a numeric or letter grade.
- Device-local persistence with no accounts or cross-device synchronization.
- Explicit consent before any uploaded text is sent to an AI service.
- The deterministic builder remains usable without AI.
- Individual club assessments stay private; public research is aggregate only.
- Educational drafting assistance, not legal advice or party approval.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is DemClubs and the public domain is demclubs.org. Voice is direct, civic, calm, bilingual, and specific. It must not resemble campaign hype, legal-tech intimidation, a generic SaaS dashboard, or a partisan fundraising page.

## Evidence on Hand

- San Diego County Democratic Party club directory and published 2026 club spreadsheet.
- 55 listed clubs, currently including 53 linked bylaws and two missing bylaws.
- County-party bylaws and club manual links.
- No approved logo, testimonials, adoption statistics, or formal legal/party endorsement; none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Decisions before prose: every generated clause traces to a club answer or governing requirement.
2. Authority is explicit: distinguish mandatory rules, safeguards, common practice, and preferences.
3. Privacy by default: local work stays local unless the user opts into enhanced analysis.
4. Alternatives have consequences: recommendations explain the operational trade, not just the wording.
5. A working draft may be incomplete, but incompleteness is always visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA expectations, support keyboard and screen-reader operation, remain usable at 200% text zoom, and provide complete English and Spanish workflows. Avoid color-only statuses and legalistic language when plain language is accurate.
